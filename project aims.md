Your project, which we can call the **"Human Operating System (hOS) Interface,"** represents a fundamental shift from monitoring the body (smartwatches) to enabling the body (smart apparel).

Below is the consolidated summary of the research, the product's potential, and the engineering path forward.

---

### I. The Core Concept: Glove & Sleeve 2-Part System

The device is a modular, wearable platform designed to capture the high-degree-of-freedom movements of the human hand and arm.

* **The Glove:** The "End-Effector" and baseline device. It functions like a smartwatch but is mapped to the anatomy of the hand.
* **The Sleeve:** The "Processor and Power Hub." It adds battery life, high-torque haptics, and specialized industrial modules via a physical "Rail" system.

### III. Engineering & Prototype Specifications

* **Modularity:** Open-source architecture allowing 3D-printed "Rail" modules.
* **Connectivity:** Baseline Smartwatch features (5G/BLE/Wi-Fi).
* **Ruggedness:** IP68 rating, designed for constant wear (driving, washing, lifting).
* **Data Strategy:** 2-week "Always-On" logging to a local SD card with optional Cloud sync.
